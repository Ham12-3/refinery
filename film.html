    Distillation

    Crude oil arrives by tanker and is pumped to storage, then heated and fed into the atmospheric distillation column. Inside, it separates by boiling point: light gases leave at the top, heavy residue at the bottom, with naphtha, kerosene and diesel drawn off in between.

      02 / 03

      Conversion

      The camera drops between the process units. Fired heaters bring the feed up to around 400 °C before it enters catalytic reactors, where hydrogen removes sulphur and heavier molecules are broken down into lighter fuels.

        03 / 03

        Storage and export

        Finished products run along the main pipe rack to the tank farm. Floating-roof tanks hold them until a tanker loads at the marine terminal, while the conversion units on the skyline keep running.
